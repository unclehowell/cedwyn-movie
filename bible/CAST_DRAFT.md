# CEDWYN — repertory cast (draft v0.1, 25 Oct 2026)

Device: a small soap REPERTORY COMPANY. The same few faces play the people of Llandough in every age
(the Williams line, the village voices, the officials). Consistency is the point: one fixed reference
portrait per actor, costume/age changes are edits of that portrait, never a new face.
Real family members are played by these actors with NO attempt at likeness until the family supplies
photos and approves. the producer is never named on screen (he is "the Grandson" / "the Researcher").
No heraldry anywhere (no crests, coats of arms, shields, armorial devices).
