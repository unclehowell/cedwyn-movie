# Mary Williams — a short film (private dramatisation)

A short dramatisation, about 5 minutes long, told from Mary Williams' point of view and following the family's account of Tŷ Mawr, Llandough, from 1939 to William Buckler's last years.

- `mary-williams.webm`: 720p VP9 with English audio (Opus) and burned-in English subtitles.
- `mary-williams.en.vtt`: the same subtitles as a sidecar file.
- `src/`: the source stills and lines used for the four OmniHuman talking close-ups.

Mary's and William's looks are drawn from the family photographs supplied for this purpose. Every other face is a fictional actor from the Cedwyn film's repertory company. This is a dramatisation, not a documentary record.
