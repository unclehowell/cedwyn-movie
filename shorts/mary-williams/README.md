# Mary Williams — a short film (private dramatisation)

A short dramatisation, about 6 minutes long, told from Mary Williams' point of view. It follows the family's account of Tŷ Mawr, Llandough, from 1939 to William Buckler's last years.

- `mary-williams.webm`: revision v3 (27 Oct 2026), 720p VP9 with English audio (Opus) and burned-in English subtitles. The address cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate stays on screen throughout, larger and higher than in v2, with the subtitles above it. The 1950 scene now follows Mary's own typed statement (catalogue GHF-E196-01): a lodger placed in the house by Mr Knapp, an estate agent of Penarth, later told her he had taken the papers from the blanket box, including the agreement relating to the farm, and given them to Mr Knapp. Evidence-catalogue panels give the record behind each statement, or say "Family account — record not yet located". A title card marks each corporate advance. A 1988 eviction scene includes real ITV/HTV Wales footage from the family's YouTube channel.
- `mary-williams.v2.webm`: revision v2, kept for comparison.
- `mary-williams.v1.webm`: the first cut (v1), kept for comparison.
- `mary-williams.en.vtt`: the v3 subtitles as a sidecar file.
- `src/`: the source stills and lines used for the four OmniHuman talking close-ups.

Mary's and William's looks are drawn from the family photographs supplied for this purpose. Branwen Buckler appears only in the real 1988 news footage, shown with the family's permission for this private film. Every other face is a fictional actor from the Cedwyn film's repertory company. This is a dramatisation, not a documentary record.
